DIGITAL MARKETING

A practical digital marketing plan for a small business

Start with the outcome

Before choosing social platforms or ad formats, decide what the business needs next. That may be qualified enquiries, store visits, product sales or a clearer online presence.

A specific goal makes it easier to decide what to publish, where to spend time and what to measure.

Understand the audience

Write down who the service is for, what problem they are trying to solve and what questions they ask before making a decision. Those questions can guide your website pages, content and campaign messages.

  • Identify the main customer and service area
  • List common questions and objections
  • Review how potential customers currently discover the business

Choose connected channels

A small business does not need to do everything at once. A well-structured service page, useful organic content and one carefully planned campaign can work together. Review the plan regularly and adjust based on real enquiries and analytics.
