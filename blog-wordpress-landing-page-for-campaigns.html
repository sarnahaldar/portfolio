Designing a WordPress landing page around a campaign

Keep the message consistent

Use the same offer and language from the campaign on the destination page. Visitors should not have to guess whether they have reached the right place.

Design for the next action

Put the essential information in a logical order: what the service is, who it helps, what is included and how to make an enquiry. Remove unnecessary navigation and distractions from a focused landing page.

  • Use a clear page heading and concise service summary
  • Make the call to action visible and descriptive
  • Keep forms short and comfortable on a phone
  • Optimize images and check responsive layouts

Review the whole journey

Before launch, test the page on common screen sizes and submit the form yourself. A useful landing page is not just a design; it is one step in a complete marketing journey.
